What's new in Atelier 0.3.0

  • New name, new icon: Gouache becomes Atelier. Your .gouache files, settings and automatic updates are kept; only the name changes (if the app in Applications is still called Gouache, you can rename it Atelier).
  • Essentiel mode: click what bothers you (sky, person, background, object) and Atelier offers three variants computed on your photo plus a slider to fine-tune. Automatic suggestions (tilted, a bit dark, subject detected), a “What I did” list where each line can be hidden, changed or removed, Space to see the original, Back to original always available.
  • On-device scene analysis: sky, people, faces, subject, background, objects and text are found on the Mac, with no network.
  • Retouching: remove an object, cut out, blur / remove / replace the background, bluer, dramatic or replaced sky, smoothed skin, brightened face, portrait light, red eyes, light, colours, auto enhance, moods, straightening, cropping, text, duplicate an object.
  • Non-destructive core: adjustment layers, masks, editable filters, styles, groups, clipping, non-destructive crop and rotation; everything stays editable in the Studio, the .gouache file keeps the original photo and the list.
  • Richer Studio: Healing, Patch, Remove object, Dodge / Burn / Sponge, Crop with straightening, Perspective and mesh, Liquify; Light, Colours, Auto enhance, Noise, Unsharp mask, Deblur adjustments; Upscale, Replace sky, Portrait, Harmonize, RAW development, histogram and clipped areas.
  • Export by destination: send by message, publish online, print, keep at maximum quality, or set everything by hand.
  • Home screen (drop a photo, templates, recents) and ⌘K palette “What do you want to do?”.
  • Light and dark mode everywhere, 7 languages.
