What's new in Atelier 0.3.1

  • Essential redesigned: a floating action bar under the photo, variants shown as cards, a clearer journal, and a help tip on every button.
  • Erase: always one click away. Brush or circle what you want gone and Atelier fills it in; “Again” offers another result. Escape clears everything, including a selection made in the Studio.
  • Help everywhere: a plain-language explanation when hovering any tool, option, menu, effect or slider; a help line at the bottom of the window; Help menu → User Guide (searchable), First Steps (guided tour), Shortcuts.
  • Studio rethought for beginners: tools grouped by intent and named, Soft / Medium / Strong presets, verb-based menus (Image → Enhance, Fix, Transform, Size), a Layers panel with named buttons, uniform dialogs with Before, presets and Reset, everyday names (technical names remain available).
  • New adjustments: HSL by color, three-way color grading, dehaze, grain, photo filter, gradient map, channel mixer, selective color, black & white by color, color transfer, equalize; 31 blend modes.
  • New filters: high pass, smart sharpen, tilt-shift, iris, field and path blurs, lens correction, content-aware scale.
  • New tools: quick selection, magnetic lasso, smudge, blur / sharpen, history brush, pattern stamp, measure (with “Straighten along the line”), full free transform (skew, distort, numeric entry); Color Range and Refine Edge windows (hair, decontamination).
  • Merge photos: HDR, focus stacking and panorama with automatic alignment. Recordable actions and batch processing on a folder. Export As… with presets, multiple sizes and live file size.
