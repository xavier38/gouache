What's new in Atelier 0.2.0

  • First public release of Atelier, a full-featured image editor for the Mac.
  • 19 tools: paintbrush (hardness, tablet pressure), pencil, eraser, clone stamp, recolor, paint bucket, gradients (including spirals), text (bold, italic, underline, strikethrough), lines and Bézier curves, 46 shapes you can edit after drawing, selections and magic wand.
  • Fill patterns and a blend mode for every tool.
  • Layers with 14 blend modes, clickable history, rulers and units (pixels, inches, centimetres).
  • 12 adjustments + Curves, 50 effects with live preview (blurs, distortions, object: drop shadow and outline, colour: dithering, artistic: mosaic, stained glass…).
  • Formats: PNG, JPEG, HEIC, AVIF, TIFF, BMP, GIF, TGA, ICO, DDS, JPEG 2000; reads WebP, JPEG XL, PSD and RAW; Atelier format keeps your layers. Printing and scanner import.
