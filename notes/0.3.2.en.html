What's new in Atelier 0.3.2

  • Erase an object: a real neural engine, on your Mac. MI-GAN ships inside the app (railings, edges, poles: the structure continues behind the erased object); “Maximum quality” downloads LaMa (196 MB) on demand for large areas and repeated patterns. Settings → Erase to choose the engine; “Again” alternates engines.
  • Straighten: you always choose the angle. A grid appears on the photo, “Automatic” offers the detected angle, the cards offer small offsets, and the Angle slider runs from −15° to +15° in tenths of a degree.
  • Studio: the toolbar has three columns (adjustable) and collapses with one click on the chevron; a tab on the left reopens it (or ⌥⌘1).
  • Credits: MI-GAN (Picsart AI Research, MIT) and LaMa (Samsung AI, Apache 2.0), trained on Places2; see About.
